Welcome. This is the home of Petr's AI School: a series of weekly sessions for colleagues at OnSinch, Shameless and friends. No programming background needed. The goal is simple to say and takes a few weeks to reach: you open [[Claude Code]], describe what you need, and get it done, whatever your job is.

## Start here

1. [[Mental models]] - the four ideas everything else builds on. Read this first.
2. [[Choosing a client]] - terminal, VS Code or desktop app: pick your window, then install it with the [[guides/index|guides]].
3. [[Your first session]] - the homework after lecture 01. Twenty minutes, any client.
4. [[Roadmap]] - what we cover week by week.
5. [[01-introduction-to-ai|Lecture 01: Introduction to AI]] - the first session, slides included.
6. [[concepts/index|Glossary]] - every term we use, explained in plain English.

Coming back after a while? [[news|What is new]] lists everything added since you last looked, newest first.

## How to use this wiki

- **Hover over a link** to see a short explanation without leaving the page. Click it to read more.
- **Search** (the magnifier top left, or press `/`) finds any page or phrase.
- The **graph** on the right shows how a page connects to others. Follow it when you are curious.
- Pages are short on purpose. One idea per page, linked to the ideas it depends on.
